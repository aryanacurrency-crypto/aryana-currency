01

Introduction

Aryana Currency ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our services.

Our Commitment
We collect only what we need to operate the exchange, verify your identity, and protect your account. We never sell your data.
02

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use our platform:

Category What We Collect Purpose
Account Data Name, email, phone, hashed password Authentication & identity
Payment Data Hesab Pay number, USDT wallet address Processing orders
Transaction Data Order details, amounts, references, timestamps Ledger & audit trail
KYC Data ID documents, selfie photos Regulatory compliance
Technical Data IP address, browser type, access times Security & fraud prevention
Legal Requirement
Collection of KYC data is a legal requirement under our operating license. We cannot provide services to users who decline to complete verification.
03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To operate your account — creating and managing your profile, processing login sessions.
  • To process orders — executing buy and sell transactions, updating balances, recording ledger entries.
  • To verify your identity (KYC) — as required by law and by our operating license.
  • To prevent fraud — detecting suspicious activity, money laundering, and unauthorized access.
  • To communicate with you — responding to support requests and sending important account notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations — responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities.

We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and you may opt out at any time.

04

How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your data seriously. Our protective measures include:

  • Password hashing — passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • HTTPS encryption — all communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted.
  • JWT authentication — sessions use short-lived tokens with automatic refresh rotation.
  • Access controls — access to customer data is restricted to authorized personnel and logged.
  • Segregated wallets — customer funds are held separately from operational funds.
  • Regular audits — our systems are regularly reviewed for vulnerabilities.
No System is 100% Secure
While we take every reasonable precaution, no online platform can guarantee absolute security. We recommend you use a strong, unique password and enable any available security features.
05

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With regulatory authorities — when legally required, such as in response to a lawful court order or regulatory inquiry.
  • With service providers — essential partners such as our cloud hosting and database providers, bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • With your explicit consent — for any other purpose, only after you have given us clear permission.

In all cases, we share only the minimum information necessary for the specific purpose.

06

Data Retention

We retain your account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and for a period required by applicable law after account closure.

Financial records are typically retained for a minimum of 5 years after the last transaction, in accordance with regulatory requirements.

After the retention period expires, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

07

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion — request deletion of your account, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Object — object to certain types of processing, such as marketing communications.
  • Right to Data Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address in the Contact section below. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to:

  • Keep you logged in between sessions
  • Remember your language preference (English, Dari, Pashto)
  • Remember your theme preference (dark or light mode)

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any cookies that follow you across other websites.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Effective date" at the top of this page
  • Announce the change prominently on our platform
  • Notify you by email if the changes significantly affect your rights

Your continued use of the service after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
