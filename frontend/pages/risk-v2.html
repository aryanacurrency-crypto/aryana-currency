Important Warning

Please Read Carefully
Trading digital currencies involves significant risk. You should never trade with funds you cannot afford to lose. The value of digital currencies can go up or down dramatically, and you may lose some or all of your investment.

This Risk Disclosure explains the principal risks associated with using Aryana Currency. It does not cover every possible risk — you should conduct your own research and seek independent advice if necessary.

01

Market Risk

The value of digital currencies such as Tether (USDT) and traditional currencies such as the Afghan Afghani (AFN) can fluctuate significantly within short periods of time.

You may gain or lose money as a result of these fluctuations. Historical performance is not indicative of future results, and no one can predict with certainty how exchange rates will move.

Rate Lock Policy
When you place an order on Aryana Currency, the rate is locked at that moment. However, if the market rate moves significantly before our team verifies the payment, the order may be cancelled at our discretion to prevent arbitrage abuse.
02

Network & Blockchain Risk

Cryptocurrency transfers occur on public blockchains, which are outside of our control. Risks include:

  • Sending funds to the wrong address. These transactions are irreversible — no one can recover them.
  • Choosing the wrong network. Sending TRC20 USDT to an ERC20 address, for example, will result in permanent loss of funds.
  • Network congestion. High demand may lead to delays or higher transaction fees.
  • Blockchain forks. In rare cases, a blockchain may split, affecting the validity of transactions.
  • Smart contract vulnerabilities. If you use a third-party wallet or exchange, its security is your responsibility.
Double-Check Before Sending
Always double-check the wallet address and network before clicking "send". Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Aryana Currency is not responsible for funds sent to incorrect addresses.
03

Payment Method Risk

AFN payments are made via Hesab Pay. You are solely responsible for:

  • Ensuring the account details you provide are correct. Incorrect details may result in funds sent to the wrong person.
  • Keeping your Hesab Pay account secure. If someone gains access to your account, they may initiate unauthorized transactions.
  • Verifying the recipient before sending funds. Always confirm the account holder name matches "ARYANA CURRENCY" before sending.
  • Keeping your transaction receipts. You will need them for reference and verification.
04

Regulatory Risk

The legal status of digital currencies varies by country and may change at any time. It is your responsibility to understand and comply with the laws of your jurisdiction.

Aryana Currency operates under a specific license. If regulatory conditions change, we may be required to:

  • Suspend certain services temporarily or permanently.
  • Restrict access for users in specific jurisdictions.
  • Report certain transactions to regulatory authorities.

We will make reasonable efforts to notify users of such changes, but we cannot guarantee advance warning.

05

Verification Risk

Every order on Aryana Currency is manually verified by our team. This step is intentional and required by law, but it introduces several risks:

  • Delays. Verification may take up to 24 hours during peak periods.
  • Rejection. If we cannot verify your payment reference, your order may be rejected.
  • Refund delays. If an order is rejected, refunds may take additional time to process.

To minimize these risks, always double-check your transaction reference before submitting it.

06

Technical Risk

Like any online platform, Aryana Currency may experience:

  • Scheduled maintenance. We may temporarily suspend services for upgrades.
  • Unexpected downtime. Technical failures may prevent access to your account.
  • Bugs and errors. Despite rigorous testing, software bugs can occur.
  • Cyber attacks. We take extensive precautions, but no platform is immune.

We work hard to maintain 99.9% uptime and respond quickly to any incidents, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service.

Your Responsibility
You are solely responsible for your trading decisions and their outcomes. Aryana Currency does not provide investment advice.
08

Best Practices

To protect yourself while using Aryana Currency, we strongly recommend:

  • Double-check wallet addresses before every send.
  • Verify the network (TRC20 for USDT) before sending.
  • Start with small amounts until you're comfortable with the platform.
  • Keep your password and 2FA secure. Never share them with anyone.
  • Beware of phishing. Always verify you're on the official website.
  • Keep transaction receipts for reference.
  • Contact support immediately if anything looks suspicious.
