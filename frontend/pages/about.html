About Aryana

The most trusted exchange
built for Afghanistan.

Aryana Currency is a licensed digital currency exchange connecting Afghanistan to the global economy — fast, secure, and transparent.

Our Mission

We believe every Afghan should have access to simple, secure, and affordable financial tools. Aryana Currency was founded to bridge the gap between Afghanistan's cash economy and the digital world — making it effortless to exchange between Afghan Afghani (AFN) and Tether (USDT).

Our mission is simple: give people a better way to move money. No hidden fees, no confusing processes, no waiting days. Just fast, transparent exchange at the best rates available.

Our Story

Aryana Currency was born from a simple frustration: exchanging money in Afghanistan was slow, expensive, and complicated. We saw families sending money home, businesses paying international suppliers, and individuals trying to save in stable currencies — all facing the same barriers.

In 2026, a small team of engineers and finance professionals came together with one goal: build an exchange that actually works for Afghanistan. We started with USDT because it's stable, widely used, and trusted. But our vision goes much further.

Today, thousands of Afghans use Aryana Currency every month. And we're just getting started.
