ABOUT US

A trusted exchange, built in Afghanistan.

Aryana Currency is a licensed digital currency exchange focused on making USDT ↔ AFN trading clear, safe and accessible for everyone.

Our Story

Aryana Currency was founded with a simple goal: give people in Afghanistan a trustworthy, modern way to exchange digital currency. For years, the market has been informal, rates have been unclear, and trust has been hard to find.

We wanted to change that. Our platform is designed around transparency, security, and a straightforward customer experience — from the rate you see, to the moment the funds arrive.

How We Work

We operate a manual-verified model. When you place an order:

  1. You send the funds (AFN via Hesab Pay, or USDT via Tron network).
  2. You submit the transaction reference in your dashboard.
  3. Our team verifies the payment — usually within minutes.
  4. We send the equivalent funds to your account.

This manual step is intentional. It's how we protect both you and the platform from fraud.
