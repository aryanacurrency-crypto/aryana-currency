01

Our Story

Aryana Currency was founded with a single, uncompromising goal: to give people in Afghanistan a trustworthy and modern way to exchange digital currency. For years, the market has been informal, rates have been opaque, and trust has been difficult to establish.

We set out to change that. Our platform is designed around three pillars — transparency, security, and simplicity — so that every customer, whether they are exchanging for the first time or the hundredth, has a clear and dependable experience.

Founding Principle
Every transaction on Aryana Currency is manually verified. We believe this human step is the foundation of trust in digital currency exchanges — especially in emerging markets.
02

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a licensed, transparent, and accessible digital currency exchange that serves the people of Afghanistan with integrity. We aim to remove the friction, uncertainty, and risk that have historically characterized the informal currency exchange market.

We measure our success not only by the volume of transactions we process, but by the trust our customers place in us — and by the standard of service we deliver every day.

05

How We Work

We operate a manual-verified model. When you place an order, the following steps occur:

  1. You send the funds. AFN payments are made via Hesab Pay. USDT payments are made via the Tron (TRC20) network.
  2. You submit the reference. In your dashboard, you enter the transaction reference or TXID.
  3. Our team verifies the payment. A member of our team confirms the payment — typically within minutes.
  4. We send the equivalent funds. Once verified, we send the equivalent amount to your account.
Why Manual Verification?
This manual step is intentional. It is how we protect both you and the platform from fraud, and it is a legal requirement under our operating license.
