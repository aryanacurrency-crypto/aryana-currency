Risk Disclosure

Important information you should understand before trading digital currency.

⚠ Please Read Carefully Trading digital currencies involves significant risk. Only trade with funds you can afford to lose.

1. Market Risk

The value of digital currencies such as Tether (USDT) and traditional currencies such as the Afghan Afghani (AFN) can fluctuate significantly. You may gain or lose money as a result of these fluctuations.

2. Network & Blockchain Risk

Cryptocurrency transfers occur on public blockchains. Risks include:

  • Sending funds to the wrong address — these transactions are irreversible.
  • Choosing the wrong network (e.g., sending TRC20 USDT to an ERC20 address) — funds may be permanently lost.
  • Network congestion leading to delays or higher fees.

3. Payment Method Risk

AFN payments are made via Hesab Pay. You are responsible for:

  • Ensuring the account details you provide are correct.
  • Keeping your Hesab Pay account secure.
  • Verifying the recipient before sending funds.

4. Regulatory Risk

The legal status of digital currencies varies by country and may change. It is your responsibility to understand and comply with the laws of your jurisdiction.

5. Verification Risk

Every order is manually verified. If we cannot verify your payment reference, your order may be delayed, rejected, or refunded.

6. Technical Risk

Like any online platform, we may occasionally experience downtime, bugs, or service interruptions. While we work hard to prevent this, we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service.

7. No Investment Advice

Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice. All trading decisions are yours alone. We do not recommend buying or selling any particular asset.

8. Best Practices

To protect yourself:

  • Always double-check wallet addresses before sending.
  • Use the correct network for your transfer.
  • Start with small amounts until you're comfortable.
  • Keep your password and 2FA secure.
  • Contact our support if anything seems unclear.

9. Acknowledgment

By using Aryana Currency, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these risks and accept full responsibility for your trading decisions.
