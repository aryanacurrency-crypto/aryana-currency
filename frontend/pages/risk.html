Understanding the Risks

Trading digital currencies (like Tether/USDT) involves significant risk. You should only trade with money you can afford to lose. Before using Aryana Currency, carefully consider the following risks.

Price Volatility
Digital currency prices can fluctuate rapidly and unpredictably. The value of your holdings may go up or down significantly.
Regulatory Risk
Laws and regulations for digital currencies vary by country and can change. Regulatory changes may affect the availability of the Service.
Security Risk
Despite our best efforts, no system is 100% secure. Always protect your passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
Transaction Delays
Blockchain transactions can occasionally take longer due to network congestion or verification processes.
Counterparty Risk
In P2P trading, you trade directly with other users. Even with escrow, some risk of disputes exists.
Human Error
Mistakes in entering wallet addresses or amounts can result in permanent loss of funds. Always verify details carefully.

Our Recommendations

  • Start with small amounts until you understand how the platform works
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your account
  • Never share your password or login credentials
  • Always double-check wallet addresses before confirming transactions
  • Keep records of all your transactions
  • Report suspicious activity to our support team immediately
