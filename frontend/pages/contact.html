Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an order take?

Most orders are verified within a few minutes during business hours. In busy periods, it may take up to 2 hours. You can track the status in your dashboard.

What is the minimum order?

10 USDT per transaction.

What are the fees?

A flat 1.5% platform fee applies to every completed order. No hidden charges.

Which network do you use for USDT?

We currently support USDT on the TRC20 (Tron) network for fast, low-cost transfers.

Is my money safe?

Yes. Funds are held in secure wallets, every payment is manually verified, and all transactions are logged in a double-entry ledger for a complete audit trail.
