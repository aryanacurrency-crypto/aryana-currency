03

Frequently Asked Questions

Before reaching out, please review the most common questions below — you may find your answer immediately.

How long does an order take?
+
Most orders are verified within minutes during business hours. In busy periods, it may take up to 2 hours. You can always track the status in your dashboard.
What is the minimum order amount?
+
The minimum order is 10 USDT per transaction. There is no maximum — trade as much as you need.
What are the fees?
+
A flat 1.5% platform fee applies to every completed order. No hidden charges, no surprises.
Which network do you support for USDT?
+
We currently support USDT on the TRC20 (Tron) network. This network offers fast confirmations and low transaction fees.
How do I verify my identity (KYC)?
+
Go to your Profile → Verification and upload a clear photo of your ID and a selfie. Our team will review and approve it — usually within 24 hours.
Is my money safe on your platform?
+
Yes. Funds are held in segregated wallets, every payment is manually verified, and all transactions are logged in a double-entry ledger for a complete audit trail.
04

Before You Contact Us

To help us resolve your issue faster, please have the following information ready before reaching out:

  • Your account email — so we can locate your account immediately.
  • Order reference number — if your question relates to a specific order (e.g., ARY-2026-A1B2C3).
  • Transaction reference or TXID — if you're asking about a payment you've made.
  • Clear description — explain what you expected and what actually happened.
  • Screenshots — if applicable, attach screenshots of the issue.
Never Share Your Password
Our team will never ask for your password, 2FA codes, or recovery phrases. If someone claiming to be from Aryana Currency asks for these, it is a scam — report it immediately.
