Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we protect it.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with the best possible service:

  • Account information: name, email, phone, country, password
  • KYC information: ID documents, selfie photos
  • Transaction data: trades, deposits, withdrawals, order history
  • Technical data: IP address, device info, browser type
  • Usage data: how you interact with our platform

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process your transactions and send related notifications
  • Verify your identity (KYC) as required by law
  • Detect and prevent fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activities
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. How We Share Your Information

We never sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Service providers who help us operate (under strict confidentiality)
  • Third parties in case of business transfer (with advance notice)

4. Security

We protect your information using:

  • Bank-grade encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Regular security audits
  • Strict access controls for our team

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion (subject to legal requirements)
  • Export your data
  • Object to certain processing

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active, plus a period required by legal obligations (typically 5 years for financial records).

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences (language, theme)
  • Analyze usage to improve the Service

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work.

8. Children's Privacy

Aryana Currency is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact our Data Protection team at privacy@aryana.af.
