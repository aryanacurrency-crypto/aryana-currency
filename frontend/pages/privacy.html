Privacy Policy

How Aryana Currency collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Summary We collect only what we need to operate the exchange, verify your identity, and protect your account. We never sell your data. We never share it except when required by law.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, password (hashed).
  • Payment data: Hesab Pay number, USDT wallet address, transaction references.
  • Transaction data: orders, amounts, timestamps, and status history.
  • KYC data (when applicable): identity documents you upload for verification.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and access times for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To operate your account and process orders.
  • To verify your identity (KYC) as required by law.
  • To prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorized access.
  • To communicate with you about your account or orders.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take security seriously. Our protections include:

  • Passwords are hashed with industry-standard bcrypt — never stored in plain text.
  • All communication uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Sessions use short-lived JWT tokens with refresh rotation.
  • Access to customer data is restricted and logged.
  • Funds are held in secure, segregated wallets.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following cases:

  • With regulatory authorities, when legally required.
  • With service providers essential to operating the platform (hosting, database), under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • With your explicit consent, for any other purpose.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and for the period required by applicable law after that (typically 5 years for financial records).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Contact us with any privacy-related concern.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you logged in and to remember your language and theme preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on the platform. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.
