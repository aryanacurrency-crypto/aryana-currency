01

Introduction

Aryana Currency is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices users have regarding their personal data.

By using the service, users agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If any part of this policy is not acceptable, please do not use the service.

Commitment
Only what is needed to operate the exchange, verify identities, and protect accounts is collected. Personal data is never sold.
02

Information Collected

The following categories of information are collected when the platform is used:

Category What Is Collected Purpose
Account Data Name, email, phone, hashed password Authentication & identity
Payment Data Hesab Pay number, USDT wallet address Processing orders
Transaction Data Order details, amounts, references, timestamps Ledger & audit trail
KYC Data ID documents, selfie photos Regulatory compliance
Technical Data IP address, browser type, access times Security & fraud prevention
Legal Requirement
Collection of KYC data is a legal requirement under the operating license. Services cannot be provided to users who decline to complete verification.
03

How Information Is Used

Information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To operate accounts — creating and managing user profiles, processing login sessions.
  • To process orders — executing buy and sell transactions, updating balances, recording ledger entries.
  • To verify identities (KYC) — as required by law and by the operating license.
  • To prevent fraud — detecting suspicious activity, money laundering, and unauthorized access.
  • To communicate — responding to support requests and sending important account notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations — responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities.

Data is not used for marketing purposes without explicit consent, and consent may be withdrawn at any time.

04

How Information Is Protected

The security of user data is taken seriously. Protective measures include:

  • Password hashing — passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • HTTPS encryption — all communication between the browser and the servers is encrypted.
  • JWT authentication — sessions use short-lived tokens with automatic refresh rotation.
  • Access controls — access to customer data is restricted to authorized personnel and logged.
  • Segregated wallets — customer funds are held separately from operational funds.
  • Regular audits — systems are regularly reviewed for vulnerabilities.
No System is 100% Secure
While every reasonable precaution is taken, no online platform can guarantee absolute security. Users are advised to use a strong, unique password and enable any available security features.
05

Sharing Information

Personal data is never sold. Information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With regulatory authorities — when legally required, such as in response to a lawful court order or regulatory inquiry.
  • With service providers — essential partners such as cloud hosting and database providers, bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • With explicit consent — for any other purpose, only after clear permission has been given.

In all cases, only the minimum information necessary for the specific purpose is shared.

06

Data Retention

Account and transaction data is retained for as long as the account is active, and for a period required by applicable law after account closure.

Financial records are typically retained for a minimum of 5 years after the last transaction, in accordance with regulatory requirements.

After the retention period expires, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

07

User Rights

The following rights are held regarding personal data:

  • Right to Access — request a copy of the personal data held.
  • Right to Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion — request account deletion, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Object — object to certain types of processing, such as marketing communications.
  • Right to Data Portability — request data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please use the Contact page. A response will be provided within 30 days.

08

Cookies & Local Storage

Browser local storage is used to:

  • Keep users logged in between sessions
  • Remember language preference (English, Dari, Pashto)
  • Remember theme preference (dark or light mode)

Third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any cookies that follow users across other websites are not used.

09

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. When material changes are made:

  • The "Effective date" at the top of this page will be updated.
  • The change will be announced prominently on the platform.
  • Registered users will be notified by email if the changes significantly affect their rights.

Continued use of the service after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
